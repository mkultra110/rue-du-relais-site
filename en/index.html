Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress stored?
On your iPhone and nowhere else. There is no account. If iCloud Backup is on in iOS, Apple may copy it along with the rest of your device.

I deleted the game. Is my progress lost?
Yes, unless you restore an iPhone backup made before you deleted it.

Does the game need the internet?
No. It is played entirely offline.

Are there purchases or ads?
No, neither.

Refunds.
The game is free. For anything else, Apple handles requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Language.
The game itself is in French.

Privacy

The game collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
