Privacy policy

Game: Hordetown, for iPhone. Last updated: October 19, 2026.

In short

Hordetown collects no data. Nothing leaves your iPhone.

Publisher

mkultra110, independent developer. Contact: github.com/mkultra110/rue-du-relais-site/issues.

Data collected

None. The game has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reports sent to the publisher and no third-party service that collects information. It does not use the internet and asks for no permission: no location, microphone, camera, contacts or ad tracking.

Data kept on your device

Your progress and settings are saved inside the game, on your iPhone. If you turned on iCloud Backup in iOS, Apple may include them in your device backup. That copy is governed by Apple and its rules, not by us.

Apple reports

If you agreed to share analytics with Apple in iOS settings, Apple may receive crash reports. We can only see them through Apple's tools, in anonymous form.

Deletion

Deleting the game erases its data from your iPhone. Since we collected nothing, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Children

The game collects no data, including from minors.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example with a new service, this page will be updated and dated before the game update that uses it.
